Send a game

  1. In your group chat, tap +, then Pickup IO.
  2. Tap Send to chat, then send.

Answer a game

  1. Tap the card, then In or Out.
  2. Bringing friends? Tap + for each guest.
  3. Owe money? Tap Pay with Venmo or Cash App, then I paid.

Run a game

  1. Open your card. Everyone is listed with an In / Out switch.
  2. Tap Mark paid or Request next to anyone who owes.

Waitlist and fines

How does the waitlist work?
Once the game hits its max, tapping In puts you on the waitlist. Your place is when you tapped.
What happens when a spot opens?
The next person in line is in. The card’s message names them, like “Ray, you’re in · Jordan dropped,” so the group chat is the alert.
Can I lose my spot?
Only if you tap Out, the organizer switches you Out, or the organizer lowers the max. Tapping Out and back In puts you at the back of the line. Guests line up behind players.
How do no-show fines work?
Fines are off unless the organizer turns them on for a game. After the game they mark who didn’t show by hand. You pay it with Venmo or Cash App, tap I paid, and you can tap In again. The organizer then confirms it.
Does a fine follow me to other groups?
No. A fine only follows you within the same group chat.
