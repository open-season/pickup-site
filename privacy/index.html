io privacy policy

Effective October 2026

Pickup IO is an iMessage app for organizing games. It does not collect, store, or share your personal information. There are no accounts, no servers of our own, and no ads. The only thing it sends anywhere is anonymous usage counts, described below.

Where your game lives

When you send a game, its details (title, time, place, price, the names and answers of people who reply, the waitlist, and any no-show fines) travel inside the iMessage itself, encrypted by Apple like any other message. Only people in that conversation can see it. Pickup IO's developer never receives a copy. If a card's link is opened in a web browser, the game details stay in the part of the link that browsers don't send to websites.

What stays on your device

Pickup IO saves your name, your usual game settings, and the latest copy of games you've opened (including any no-show fines in them), so the card stays up to date. This stays on your iPhone and is deleted when you delete IO.

Calendar

If you tap Add to Calendar, iOS shows its own event screen with the game filled in, and you choose whether to add it. Pickup IO doesn't ask for calendar access and cannot read your calendar.

Payments

Pickup IO does not handle money. Pay buttons open Venmo or Cash App with an amount filled in. Those payments follow Venmo's and Cash App's own privacy policies.

Anonymous usage counts

To learn which features get used, Pickup IO sends anonymous usage counts to TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service. A count says something like "a game was created (paid, fines off)" or "a pay link was opened", along with the app version, iOS version, device model and language. To tell devices apart it uses a one-way hashed device identifier that can't be traced back to you. It never includes names, phone numbers, chat contents, game titles, places, amounts or anything you type. These counts are not linked to your identity, not used for tracking or advertising, and not shared with anyone else.

Children

Pickup IO is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: jason@openseason.org.
