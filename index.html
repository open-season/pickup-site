FAQ

Good questions.

How does the waitlist work?

Once the game is full, tapping In puts you in line. When someone drops, the next person is in, and the card’s message names them: “Ray, you’re in · Jordan dropped.”

How do no-show fines work?

They’re optional and off unless the organizer turns them on for a game. The organizer marks who didn’t show, and they pay the fine before they can tap In again in that group chat.

What about friends on Android?

They can’t open the card. The roster and pay reminders go out as plain text, so they still see them.

Why paste the group’s names?

Messages doesn’t share names with apps. Paste them once to see who hasn’t answered.

Does Pickup IO count “in” typed in the chat?

No, apps can’t read the chat. The organizer can mark that person In.

Does Pickup IO know who paid?

No. Pickup IO never touches money, so “paid” is self-reported.

What does it cost?

Nothing. Pickup IO is free, for iPhone.
